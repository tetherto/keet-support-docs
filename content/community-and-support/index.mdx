## Where can I look for help if I get stuck?

* If the issue you’re facing prevents you from opening Keet or joining groups, please email us at `support@holepunch.to`
* Follow Keet on social media platforms like [Twitter](https://x.com/keet_io) to get help and interact with other users.

### Keet Welcome group chat

Search for **Keet Welcome** in Keet and join directly.

<Callout type="info">
Community group for general questions and discussion for Keet & Pears 👋
</Callout>

## How can I provide feedback or suggest improvements for Keet?

To provide feedback or report a bug, follow these [steps](/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/submit-a-report).
Also, you can report a bug in the following Keet group:

### Bug Bandits group chat

Search for **Bug Bandits** in Keet and join directly.

<Callout type="info">
Please use this group chat only for reporting bugs, providing feedback or Keet suggestions 🐞
</Callout>

## Where can I find the latest announcements about Keet?

You can find the latest news and updates about Keet and Pear Runtime in the Keet News group. To join, go to **Profile** > **Keet News**, or search for it directly.

### Keet News group chat

Search for **Keet News** in Keet and join directly.

<Callout type="info">
Stay tuned! Keet is evolving faster than ever! 🚀
</Callout>
