## How do I turn on Bluetooth discovery?

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

1. Go to '**Settings**' and select '**Networking**':

   ![Go to 'Settings' and select 'Networking':.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/chat-over-bluetooth/desktop/settings-menu.png "Chat over Bluetooth 📡 · Desktop")
2. Toggle on '**Enable Bluetooth discovery**':

   ![Toggle on 'Enable Bluetooth discovery':.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/chat-over-bluetooth/desktop/networking-toggle-off.png "Chat over Bluetooth 📡 · Desktop")
3. The first time you enable it, your device asks for permission to use Bluetooth. Allow it to continue:

   ![The first time you enable it, your device asks for permission to use Bluetooth. Allow it to continue:.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/chat-over-bluetooth/desktop/allow-bluetooth-permission.png "Chat over Bluetooth 📡 · Desktop")
4. '**Enable Bluetooth discovery**' is now on:

   ![‘Enable Bluetooth discovery' is now on.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/chat-over-bluetooth/desktop/networking-toggle-on.png "Chat over Bluetooth 📡 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

1. Go to '**Settings**' and select '**Networking**':

   ![Go to 'Settings' and select 'Networking':.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/chat-over-bluetooth/mobile/settings-menu.png "Chat over Bluetooth 📡 · Mobile")
2. Toggle on '**Enable Bluetooth discovery**':

   ![Toggle on 'Enable Bluetooth discovery':.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/chat-over-bluetooth/mobile/networking-toggle-off.png "Chat over Bluetooth 📡 · Mobile")
3. The first time you enable it, your device asks for permission to use Bluetooth. Allow it to continue:

   ![The first time you enable it, your device asks for permission to use Bluetooth. Allow it to continue:.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/chat-over-bluetooth/mobile/allow-bluetooth-permission.png "Chat over Bluetooth 📡 · Mobile")
4. '**Enable Bluetooth discovery**' is now on:

   ![‘Enable Bluetooth discovery' is now on.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/chat-over-bluetooth/mobile/networking-toggle-on.png "Chat over Bluetooth 📡 · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info">
📡 Bluetooth discovery works alongside your normal internet connection - turning it on doesn't disable or replace regular networking. Both people need '**Enable Bluetooth discovery**' turned on for their devices to find each other.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
If you don't allow the Bluetooth permission, discovery won't be shown as active. You can grant it later from your device's app permission settings and try again.
</Callout>

## How do I find and message someone nearby?

Once discovery is on, a '**Bluetooth discovery enabled**' banner appears at the top of your chat list with a '**Find people nearby**' button:

![Once discovery is on, a 'Bluetooth discovery enabled' banner appears at the top of your chat list with a 'F….](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/chat-over-bluetooth/mobile/find-people-nearby-banner.png "Chat over Bluetooth 📡")

You can also get there anytime from '**Start chat**' → '**People nearby**', without waiting for that banner.

Either way, Keet shows who you're discoverable as, then searches for nearby people with a '**Looking for nearby users…**' state - keep Keet open and make sure both devices stay unlocked with Bluetooth on. Each result shows their name and status (or '**Resolving…**' while Keet is still identifying them), and results are capped to the 8 nearest people by signal strength:

* If you already have that person saved as a contact, you'll see a '**Message**' button - it opens your existing DM if you have one, otherwise it starts a normal DM request.
* If they're not a saved contact yet, you'll see an '**Add**' button instead, to add them as a contact first.

Once you're messaging, you can exchange text messages with no internet needed on either side.

<Callout type="info">
There's no room or topic selection in this version - nearby discovery uses one shared, global context. If you'd rather not be found, select '**Disable Bluetooth discovery**' from the bottom of the nearby-people screen, or turn the Networking toggle off.
</Callout>

## Can I keep chatting over Bluetooth if I lose internet later?

Yes. If you go offline in an existing DM, Keet shows a prompt to continue that conversation over Bluetooth:

![If you go offline in an existing DM, Keet shows a prompt to continue that conversation over Bluetooth:.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/chat-over-bluetooth/mobile/offline-connect-over-bluetooth-banner.png "Chat over Bluetooth 📡")

As long as Bluetooth discovery is already on, you don't need to repeat nearby discovery to keep messaging someone you're already chatting with - going offline or opening the nearby list again doesn't require turning discovery on a second time.

<Callout type="warn">
Group chats, calls, file transfers, and background nearby-user notifications over Bluetooth aren't supported yet.
</Callout>
