Anyone can change their display name or avatar at any time, which bad actors could use to impersonate trusted people and run phishing or impersonation scams in public groups.
To help you tell real connections from spoofed ones, Keet shows a relationship label next to a member's name based on your [Contact Book](/file-sharing-and-messages/contact-book).

## What the labels mean

Keet shows one of four labels, depending on your relationship to the member:

* **Contact** - this person is saved in your Contact Book
* **1st** - you have a DM with this person, but they are not saved in your Contact Book
* **2nd** - this person is a contact of one of your contacts
* No label - you have no connection to this person; treat them as an unverified stranger

<Callout type="info">
These labels only appear in **public rooms and DM requests**, where you are more likely to run into people you don't already know.
</Callout>

## Where you'll see a label

### Next to a name in the member list

In a public room or large group, tap or click a member's avatar or name to see their label right away, before you even open their profile card.

### In a DM chat

If you already have a saved contact who has started a chat with you, their label appears next to their name in the chat header.

### On the profile card

Tap or click on a member's avatar or username to open their **profile card**. Their label appears next to their name at the top.

If the person is already a saved contact, the card also shows the **alias** and **note** you saved for them, along with the option to delete the contact.

### In DM requests

When someone outside your contacts sends you a DM request, Keet shows a notification you can review before replying.

<Callout type="warn">
A matching name or avatar is not proof of identity. Always check the label and, when in doubt, verify the person's `@username` before trusting a message from a public room.
</Callout>
