## Why does Keet say a video may not play on all devices?

Some videos are recorded with a codec (commonly **H.265 / HEVC**) that isn't supported on every device or operating system. If a peer's device can't decode that codec, the video simply won't play for them.

Keet can't fix this on the receiving end, so instead it offers to convert the video to a widely compatible format **before you send it** - as long as your own device can play the video.

<Callout type="info">
Conversion happens on your device before sending. Peers who already support the original codec aren't affected either way. 🎞️
</Callout>

## Convert a video before sending

When you attach a video Keet has detected as potentially incompatible, a banner appears above the message field.

### One incompatible video

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

The banner reads '**This video may not play on all devices**'. Select '**Convert video**' to create a compatible version.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

The banner reads '**This video may not play on all devices**'. Tap '**Convert video**' to create a compatible version.

![This video may not play on all devices - with a 'Convert video' button.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/convert-incompatible-videos/mobile/banner-single-video.png "Convert Incompatible Videos 🎞️ · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

### Multiple incompatible videos

If you attach several videos at once, affected ones are marked with a warning badge, and the banner covers all of them together.

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

The banner reads '**Some videos may not play on all devices**'. Select '**Convert videos**' to convert every flagged video at once.

![Some videos may not play on all devices - flagged thumbnails and a 'Convert videos' button.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/convert-incompatible-videos/desktop/banner-multiple-videos.png "Convert Incompatible Videos 🎞️ · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

The banner reads '**Some videos may not play on all devices**'. Tap '**Convert videos**' to convert every flagged video at once.

![Some videos may not play on all devices - flagged thumbnails and a 'Convert videos' button.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/convert-incompatible-videos/mobile/banner-multiple-videos.png "Convert Incompatible Videos 🎞️ · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info">
Don't want to convert? Dismiss the banner [X] and the video sends unaltered - peers whose devices support the original codec will still be able to play it normally.
</Callout>

## When a video is too large to convert

Keet can only convert videos up to **5 minutes** long. If a video runs longer than that, Keet lets you know, and you can send it as is or trim it outside Keet and try again.

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

<Callout type="warn">
Sending a video without converting it doesn't break anything - it's simply not guaranteed to play on every peer's device. ⚠️
</Callout>
