## What happens when I send a link?

You don't need to wait for a preview before sending. Paste a link, send your message, and keep chatting. Keet sends the message straight away, then fetches the link's preview in the background. A second or two later, the preview appears under your message in the chat.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

* The preview is added to the message you already sent. Keet doesn't mark the message as '**edited**' when it adds a preview.
* Fetching a preview never delays sending and never freezes the app.
* If a link has no preview, or Keet can't fetch it, your message stays as a plain link. Keet doesn't show an error.

<Callout type="info">
🔗 A link in your message box can already show a preview before you send. If you dismiss that preview before sending, Keet doesn't add one afterwards.
</Callout>

## How do I remove a link preview?

You can remove a preview from your own messages. The link itself stays in the message.

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

Hover over the preview and click the '**X**' ('**Remove preview**') in its top-right corner.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

Tap the '**X**' ('**Remove preview**') in the corner of the preview.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Once you remove a preview, the message shows '**(edited)**':

![After you remove a preview, the link stays and the message shows edited.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/link-previews/desktop/preview-removed-edited.png "Link Previews 🔗 · Desktop")

You can't remove a preview from someone else's message.

## How many previews can a message show?

A message shows up to five link previews. If you send more links than that in one message, the rest stay as plain links.

## Why didn't my link get a preview?

* The website doesn't offer a preview, or Keet couldn't reach it in time.
* You dismissed the preview in the message box before sending.
* Attachments are turned off in the group.

In each case the link still works as normal.
