To send your location from a mobile device, tap '**+**' and select '**Share location'**.

Please grant access to your device’s location services, if you agree.<br />

![Please grant access to your device’s location services, if you agree.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/location-sharing/please-grant-access-device-location.jpg "Location Sharing 🌍")

Tap '**Send'** to share it, or '**Refresh'** to update the location before sending.

![Tap 'Send' to share it, or 'Refresh' to update the location before sending.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/location-sharing/send-share-refresh-update-location-2.jpg "Location Sharing 🌍")

Your current location will appear in the chat, and your peers can open it directly in their maps app.

![Your current location will appear in the chat, and your peers can open it directly in their maps app.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/location-sharing/current-location-will-appear-chat-3.png "Location Sharing 🌍")
