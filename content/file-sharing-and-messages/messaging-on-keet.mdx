### How can I send a message to a user?

* [regular groups](/keet-groups/create-a-group) - for group chats and collaboration
* [broadcast feeds](/keet-groups/create-a-group) - for one-way announcements, updates or channel-like activities
* [direct messages](/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet) -  for private, one-to-one conversations

To start messaging, you need to join an existing group, create one or initiate a DM with another peer.

### Contacting a peer

Here are the ways you can start a DM:

* **Find by username**

Start typing a member’s username in the search box to open their profile and click '**DM Request'**:

![Find by username.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/find-username.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

* **From a user profile**

Send a DM request directly from the profile panel of any member you find in a public group:

![From a user profile.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/user-profile-2.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

* **Share your @username**

Copy and send your username to someone. 

![Share your @username.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/share-username-3.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

Ask your contact to insert the link into the search box in their Keet app:

![Share your @username.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/share-username-4.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

* **Share your profile with a QR code**

Generate your QR code in '**Profile'** / '**Profile QR Code'** \[🏁]:

![Share your profile with a QR code.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/share-profile-qr-code-5.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

![Share your profile with a QR code.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/share-profile-qr-code-6.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

Ask your contact to scan this QR code in their Keet app by clicking '**Scan QR code**' \[🏁] on a mobile device (or upload the **QR image**):

![Share your profile with a QR code.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/share-profile-qr-code-7.jpg "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

### DM request

Write an invite message to share more information about yourself with the person you’re contacting. 

![Write an invite message to share more information about yourself with the person you’re contacting.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/write-invite-message-share-more-8.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

<Callout type="info">
The person will only see your initial invite message. 1️⃣ 

You can write more messages after the DM request is sent, but the user can only see them after they accept the DM.
</Callout>

Depending on the option you choose, you or your contact will receive a DM request. These always appear at the top of your groups list:

![Depending on the option you choose, you or your contact will receive a DM request. These always appear at t….](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/depending-option-choose-contact-will-9.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

You can review each DM request and choose to '**Accept'** or '**Ignore'**:

![You can review each DM request and choose to 'Accept' or 'Ignore':.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/review-each-dm-request-choose-10.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

![You can review each DM request and choose to 'Accept' or 'Ignore':.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/review-each-dm-request-choose-11.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

* Once you **accept** ✅ the request, the conversation will appear in your chat list. 
  You can now chat privately and place voice/video calls:

![Once you accept ✅ the request, the conversation will appear in your chat list.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/once-accept-request-conversation-will-12.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

* If you **ignore** ❌, all future messages from that user are going to be blocked.
* If you change your mind later, you can still send a request to them to start chatting.

![If you change your mind later, you can still send a request to them to start chatting.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/change-mind-later-still-send-13.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

### Leaving DM conversations

You can[ leave a chat with a peer](/keet-groups/leave-group) at any time and create a new one later.
To leave, open the '**Group options'** and select **‘Leave DM’** at the bottom.

Before leaving, you’ll be asked to confirm that all media files and chat history will be deleted from your device. After leaving, you won’t be able to reopen this chat.

![Before leaving, you’ll be asked to confirm that all media files and chat history will be deleted from your ….](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/before-leaving-ll-asked-confirm-14.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

<Callout type="warn">
This action is **permanent** - once you press **‘Leave Group’**, the chat will disappear from your chat list and cannot be restored. ⛔
</Callout>

Your peer will still have read-only access to the existing messages, but won’t be able to send new ones.

Please note that you **cannot send another DM invite** to the same peer until you’ve left the current DM chat.

To start a new DM, both participants need to leave the old chat first, then [send an invite](/keet-groups/joining-groups) or [scan a QR code](/keet-groups/joining-groups) to reconnect.

![To start a new DM, both participants need to leave the old chat first, then send an invite or scan a QR cod….](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/start-new-dm-both-participants-15.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

### **1-to-1** group

Regular groups are highly customizable —  unlike DMs, you can choose to enable or disable calls, messaging, or user activity notifications.
While direct messages support the call-to-ring feature, 1-to-1 groups currently don’t.

You can also assign member roles and moderate regular groups as your network grows.

![You can also assign member roles and moderate regular groups as your network grows.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet/assign-member-roles-moderate-regular-16.png "Messaging on Keet ✉️")

### Can I reply to a particular message on Keet?

Yes, to reply to a specific message in Keet:

* On Desktop, click the three dots '\[...]' on the right top of the message / select '**Reply'**.
* On Mobile, long-tap the message / tap '**Reply'** or swipe the message to the left.

### Why does a message appear later but have an earlier timestamp?

In Keet, if a message is sent while a peer is offline or experiencing connection issues, it may not reach the other party immediately. 

Once the peer is back online, the message will appear in the chat with its original send time, not the time it was delivered.

<Callout type="info">
This keeps the conversation timeline accurate, even if delivery was delayed. ⌚
</Callout>
