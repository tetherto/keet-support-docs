## How do I mute a specific chat?

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

Select '**...**' next to a chat, then '**Mute**', and choose how long to mute it for:

![Select '...' next to a chat, then 'Mute', and choose how long to mute it for.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/mute-a-chat/desktop/mute-duration-picker.png "Mute a Chat 🔇 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

Long-press a chat, then tap '**Mute**', and choose how long to mute it for:

![Long-press a chat, then tap 'Mute', and choose how long to mute it for.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/mute-a-chat/mobile/chat-context-menu.png "Mute a Chat 🔇 · Mobile")

![Choose how long to mute it for.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/mute-a-chat/mobile/mute-duration-picker-mobile.png "Mute a Chat 🔇 · Mobile")

You can also swipe left on a chat for a quicker '**Mute**' shortcut, or mute it from that chat's '**Group info**' / '**Notifications**' toggle:

<div>![You can also swipe left on a chat for a quicker 'Mute' shortcut.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/mute-a-chat/mobile/swipe-mute-action.png "Mute a Chat 🔇 · Mobile") ![Or mute it from that chat's 'Group info' / 'Notifications' toggle.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/mute-a-chat/mobile/group-info-notifications.png "Mute a Chat 🔇 · Mobile")</div>

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Choose **Forever**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, **2 hours**, **12 hours**, or **1 day**. A muted chat shows a small muted icon next to it in your chat list:

![A muted chat shows a small muted icon next to it in your chat list.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/mute-a-chat/desktop/muted-indicator.png "Mute a Chat 🔇 · Desktop")

## What happens while a chat is muted?

While a timed or forever mute is active on a chat, Keet pauses push and in-app/toast notifications for that chat only.

<Callout type="info">
🔇 Muting a chat only pauses its alerts - messages still arrive and sync normally, so you'll see everything as usual once you open it.
</Callout>

A timed mute ends on its own once the selected period is up - you don't need to do anything. You can also stop it early at any time.

<Callout type="warn">
Your device's own notification settings take priority over this setting. If notifications for Keet are disabled at the OS level, muting or unmuting a chat in-app won't change that.
</Callout>

## How do I unmute a chat, or stop a mute early?

Use the same '**Mute**' entry point - swipe, long-press, or the '**...**' menu - and select '**Unmute**':

![Use the same 'Mute' entry point - swipe, long-press, or the '...' menu - and select 'Unmute'.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/mute-a-chat/mobile/unmute-context-menu.png "Mute a Chat 🔇 · Mobile")

## Does muting a chat sync across my linked devices?

Yes. Since it's saved to your profile, muting or unmuting a chat propagates to any [linked device](/installation-and-setup/link-devices) using the same profile. If a linked device is running an older version that doesn't support timed mute yet, it falls back to muting that chat indefinitely instead.

<Callout type="info">
Looking to pause notifications for every chat at once instead of just one? See [Snooze Notifications](/file-sharing-and-messages/snooze-notifications).
</Callout>
