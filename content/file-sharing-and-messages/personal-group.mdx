## Can I create a group to save personal notes and files?

Yes. You can create a room with your saved messages and favourites, where you’re the only participant — great for keeping personal notes and sharing files across your devices.

## Create a group

Click '**Start chat' \[📝] button on the top of the screen

![Click 'Start chat' 📝 button on the top of the screen.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/personal-group/create-group-start-chat-button-top-screen.png "Personal Group 📂 · Create a group")

### In the pop-up, select 'New group chat'

![Keet app screenshot: In the pop-up, select 'New group chat'.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/personal-group/pop-up-new-group-screenshot-2.png "Personal Group 📂 · In the pop-up, select 'New group chat'")

### Add details for your new chat

![Keet app screenshot: Add details for your new chat.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/personal-group/add-details-new-chat-screenshot-3.png "Personal Group 📂 · Add details for your new chat")

### Explore possibilities

![Keet app screenshot: Explore possibilities.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/personal-group/explore-possibilities-screenshot-4.png "Personal Group 📂 · Explore possibilities")

Once the group is created, you become its administrator. Now you can leave notes for yourself and upload files that will sync across your linked devices.

<Callout type="info">
🟢 Since it’s peer-to-peer, both devices need to be online for file transfer.
</Callout>

## How can I speed up the file transfer?

Keep the chat open on both devices while transferring files.

<Callout type="info">
Upload speed depends on your internet connection. 📡
</Callout>
