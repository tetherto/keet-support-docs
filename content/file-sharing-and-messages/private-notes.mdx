## What is a private note?

A private note is a short piece of text you attach to a contact in your [Contact Book](/file-sharing-and-messages/contact-book). Use it to remember details that Keet doesn't show, like how you met, where they work, or their real name.

* Only you can see your notes. The contact and everyone else in your chats can't see them.
* A note can be up to **280 characters** long.
* Notes are for saved contacts. If someone isn't in your Contact Book yet, [add them first](/file-sharing-and-messages/contact-book#how-to-add-a-contact).

A note isn't the same as an [alias](/file-sharing-and-messages/contact-book#giving-a-contact-an-alias). An alias changes the name you see for a contact, while a note is free text that appears alongside it. You can use both.

## How do I add or edit a note?

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

Open the contact's profile, from your Contact Book or from a group's member list. Their '**User Profile**' shows an '**Alias**' row and a '**Note**' row. Select '**Note**':

![On a contact's User Profile, select Note to add a private note.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/private-notes/desktop/profile-note-tile.png "Private Notes 📝 · Desktop")

Type your note on the '**Contact remarks**' page. Keet saves it when you go back or press **Enter**. To add a new line, press **Shift** + **Enter**.

![Type your note in the Note field on the Contact remarks page.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/private-notes/desktop/note-field.png "Private Notes 📝 · Desktop")

You can also add a note from the Contact Book. Go to '**Profile**' / '**Contacts**', open the three-dot menu next to the contact, and choose '**Add remarks**'. Type in '**Note**' and select '**Save**':

![Add remarks lets you set an alias and a note for a contact, then select Save.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/private-notes/desktop/add-remarks-modal.png "Private Notes 📝 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

1. Go to '**Profile**' / '**Contacts**', tap the three-dot menu next to the contact, and choose '**Add remarks**':

   ![Tap the three-dot menu next to a contact and choose Add remarks.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/private-notes/mobile/contact-options-add-remarks.png "Private Notes 📝 · Mobile")
2. Enter your note in '**Note**' and tap '**Save**':

   ![Enter your note in the Note field and tap Save.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/private-notes/mobile/add-remarks-sheet-with-note.png "Private Notes 📝 · Mobile")

You can also open a contact's profile from a group's member list and tap the '**Note**' row:

![On a contact's User profile in a group, tap the Note row.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/private-notes/mobile/profile-note-row.png "Private Notes 📝 · Mobile")

Add your note on the '**Contact remarks**' screen and tap '**Save**' in the top right corner:

![The Contact remarks screen has an Alias field and a Note field.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/private-notes/mobile/contact-remarks.png "Private Notes 📝 · Mobile")

<Callout type="info">
The '**Note**' row appears on a contact's profile when you open it from a group's member list. If you open the same profile from your Contacts list, use '**Add remarks**' from the three-dot menu instead.
</Callout>

</Tab>
</Tabs>

If your note is longer than 280 characters, Keet shows a character-limit message ('**The character limit is 280.**' on desktop, '**Max 280 char.**' on mobile) and won't save it until you shorten it.

![On mobile, a note over 280 characters shows Max 280 char. and Save is disabled.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/private-notes/mobile/note-over-limit.png "Private Notes 📝 · Mobile")

## Where do I see my notes?

A contact's note appears in three places:

* Under their name in your **Contacts** list.
* On their profile, in the '**Note**' row.
* In the contact's options on mobile, under their name.

<Tab value="Desktop">

On a profile, the '**Note**' row shows the start of your note on one line. Select it to read the whole note:

## How do I change or delete a note?

* **To change a note**, follow the same steps and edit the text.
* **To delete a note**, clear the '**Note**' field (select the **✕** button in the field) and save.

Keet saves a note without asking you to confirm, and it doesn't show a confirmation message afterwards.

<Callout type="warn">
Deleting a contact also erases their alias and note. If you add the contact again later, you start with an empty note.
</Callout>

## What stays private?

* **Only you can see a note.** Keet doesn't show it to the contact, and it isn't part of anything you send to other people.
* **Notes aren't included when you [share a contact](/file-sharing-and-messages/share-contacts).** The card only carries the contact's public profile.
* **Notes are saved with your contacts**, in your private profile data, like the rest of your [Contact Book](/file-sharing-and-messages/contact-book).
* **Blocking a contact hides their note.** The '**Alias**' and '**Note**' options aren't available while a contact is blocked. If you [unblock](/security-and-privacy/blocked-members) them later, your note is still there.

<Callout type="info">
If you don't see the '**Note**' field, make sure Keet is up to date.
</Callout>
