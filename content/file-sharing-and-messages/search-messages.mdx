### How can I find a particular message in Keet?

To find a specific message, click '🔎' icon and enter the text you’re looking for.

![To find a specific message, click '🔎' icon and enter the text you’re looking for.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/search-messages/find-specific-message-icon-enter.png "Search Messages 🔎")

Once a match is found, you can jump straight to that message in the chat.

![Once a match is found, you can jump straight to that message in the chat.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/search-messages/once-match-found-jump-straight-2.png "Search Messages 🔎")
