## Where do I find GIFs?

GIFs live next to emojis. Open a chat, select the emoji button in the message box, and switch to the '**GIFs**' tab.

GIF search is powered by **KLIPY**. Type in the '**Search KLIPY**' field to find a GIF, or browse by the categories under the search field.

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

Click the emoji button (😊) next to the message box, then click '**GIFs**':

![The GIFs tab next to Emoji, with the Search KLIPY field, categories, and a grid of GIFs.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/send-gifs/desktop/gif-picker.png "Send GIFs 😹 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

Tap the emoji button (😊) in the message box, then tap '**GIFs**' at the bottom of the panel.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info">
GIFs need Keet 4.26 or later. Everyone in the chat should update to see the GIFs you send.
</Callout>

## How do I send a GIF?

Click or tap a GIF. Keet sends it to the chat straight away, with no extra step, and closes the picker.

Sent GIFs play in the chat and have a small '**GIF**' label. In the chat list, they show as '**Shared a GIF**'.

![GIFs play in the chat with a GIF label, with or without a caption.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/send-gifs/desktop/gif-received.png "Send GIFs 😹 · Desktop")

## How do I preview a GIF or add a caption?

To look at a GIF more closely before you send it, **right-click** it on desktop or **long-press** it on mobile. Keet shows a bigger preview with these options:

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

* '**Send**' sends the GIF.
* '**Add caption**' puts the GIF in the message box so you can write a caption, then send it like a normal message.
* '**Add to favourites**' saves the GIF to your favourites. If it's already there, you see '**Remove from favourites**' instead.

![Right-click a GIF to preview it and choose Add to favourites, Add caption, or Send.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/send-gifs/desktop/gif-preview.png "Send GIFs 😹 · Desktop")

![After Add caption, the GIF waits in the message box while you type a caption.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/send-gifs/desktop/gif-caption.png "Send GIFs 😹 · Desktop")

To close the preview without sending anything, click outside it or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>. Whatever you already typed in the message box stays there.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

* '**Send with caption**' puts the GIF in the message box so you can write a caption, then send it like a normal message.
* '**Add to favourites**' saves the GIF to your favourites. If it's already there, you see '**Remove from favourites**' instead.
* In '**Recent**' and '**Favourites**', '**Delete**' removes the GIF from that list.

To send the GIF without a caption, close the preview and tap the GIF. To close the preview without sending anything, tap outside it. Whatever you already typed in the message box stays there.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Where are my favourite and recent GIFs?

The first two icons under the search field are your own lists:

* **Recent** (🕒) shows the GIFs you've sent.
* **Favourites** (☆) shows the GIFs you saved with '**Add to favourites**'. You can save up to 100.

![The Recent list shows the GIFs you've sent.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/send-gifs/desktop/gif-recent.png "Send GIFs 😹 · Desktop")

Both lists are saved on your device and are still there the next time you open the picker. They don't sync to your other linked devices.

## Is GIF search private?

GIF search uses KLIPY's service, so the words you search for and the GIFs you browse are sent to KLIPY to show you results. The GIFs you send travel to the chat peer-to-peer, like any other file in Keet.

## What if GIFs don't load?

If you see '**Can't reach KLIPY**', check your internet connection and select '**Try again**'. Your recent GIFs still work while KLIPY is unreachable.

If a GIF won't send, wait a moment and try again. GIFs can't be sent in groups where an Admin has turned off file sharing.
