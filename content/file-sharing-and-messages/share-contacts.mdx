## What does sharing a contact do?

When you share a contact, Keet sends a **contact card** in your direct message (DM). The card shows the contact's avatar, name, and username. The person you sent it to can add that contact to their own [Contact Book](/file-sharing-and-messages/contact-book) with one tap, or simply ignore the card.

Keet doesn't ask the person whose contact you share for approval, and sharing doesn't start a chat with them. It works like passing on a name from your phone's contact app.

<Callout type="info">
You can only share contacts in a **direct message**. The option isn't available in groups. You and the person you're chatting with both need Keet 4.25 or later.
</Callout>

## How do I share contacts?

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

1. Open a DM and click the **+** button next to the message box, then choose '**Contact**':

   ![In a DM, click the + button and choose Contact.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/share-contacts/desktop/attach-menu-contact.png "Share Contacts 📇 · Desktop")
2. In '**Share contacts**', search by name or username, or pick from the list. You can select more than one contact:

   ![Select the contacts you want to share.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/share-contacts/desktop/share-contacts-picker.png "Share Contacts 📇 · Desktop")
3. Click '**Share**' to send them. The button shows how many contacts you've selected, for example '**Share (2)**':

   ![Click Share to send the selected contacts.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/share-contacts/desktop/share-contacts-selected.png "Share Contacts 📇 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

1. Open a DM and tap the **+** button next to the message box, then choose '**Contact**':

   ![In a DM, tap the + button and choose Contact.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/share-contacts/mobile/attach-menu-contact.png "Share Contacts 📇 · Mobile")
2. In '**Share contacts**', search by name or username, or pick from the list. You can select more than one contact:

   ![Select the contacts you want to share.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/share-contacts/mobile/share-contacts-sheet.png "Share Contacts 📇 · Mobile")
3. Tap '**Share 1 contact**' (or '**Share N contacts**' if you picked several) to send them:

   ![Tap Share to send the selected contacts.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/share-contacts/mobile/share-contacts-selected.png "Share Contacts 📇 · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Keet sends each contact as its own card, so sharing two contacts puts two cards in the chat. The list doesn't include the person you're chatting with, or contacts you've [blocked](/security-and-privacy/blocked-members).

<Callout type="info">
Want to share your own profile instead? See [Messaging on Keet](/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet) to share your username or QR code.
</Callout>

## What does the other person see?

In the chat, they see a card with the contact's avatar, name, and username, and an '**Add**' button. If they already have that contact saved, the button reads '**Added**' instead.

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

![The person you shared with sees a contact card with an Add button.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/share-contacts/desktop/contact-card-received.png "Share Contacts 📇 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

![The person you shared with sees a contact card with an Add button.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/share-contacts/mobile/contact-card-received.png "Share Contacts 📇 · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

* If they select '**Add**', Keet saves the contact to their Contact Book and shows '**Added to your contact list**'. The button changes to '**Added**'.
* If they don't, nothing happens. The card stays in the chat and doesn't interrupt the conversation.
* On desktop, once the contact is in their Contact Book, selecting the name or avatar on the card opens the contact's profile.

![Each shared contact arrives as its own card.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/share-contacts/desktop/contact-cards-multiple.png "Share Contacts 📇 · Desktop")

In the chat list and in replies, a shared contact shows as '**Shared a contact**'. You can reply to a contact card or pin it.

## What stays private?

* **Your [alias](/file-sharing-and-messages/contact-book#giving-a-contact-an-alias) and [private note](/file-sharing-and-messages/private-notes) for a contact are never included.** The card only carries the contact's public profile.
* **The person whose contact you share isn't contacted.** Only the person you're chatting with receives the card.
