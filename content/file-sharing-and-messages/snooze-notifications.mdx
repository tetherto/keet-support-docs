## How do I snooze all notifications in Keet?

If you need a break from alerts, you can snooze all notifications for a set period instead of muting each chat one by one.

<Callout type="info">
🔕 Snoozing notifications only pauses alerts. Message delivery, message sync, room updates, and app usage all continue as normal.
</Callout>

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

1. Go to '**Profile**' and select '**Notifications and sounds**':

   ![1. Go to 'Profile' and select 'Notifications and sounds':.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/snooze-notifications/desktop/go-profile-select-notifications-sounds.png "Snooze Notifications 🔕 · Desktop")
2. Toggle on '**Snooze all notifications**' to open the duration list:

   ![2. Toggle on 'Snooze all notifications' to open the duration list:.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/snooze-notifications/desktop/toggle-snooze-all-notifications-duration-2.png "Snooze Notifications 🔕 · Desktop")
3. Choose how long to snooze for - **Forever**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, **2 hours**, **12 hours**, or **1 day**:

   ![3. Choose how long to snooze for - Forever, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 12 hours, or 1 day:.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/snooze-notifications/desktop/choose-how-long-snooze-duration-3.png "Snooze Notifications 🔕 · Desktop")
4. '**Notifications and sounds**' now shows the active snooze and the time it ends.

   ![4. 'Notifications and sounds' now shows the active snooze and the time it ends.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/snooze-notifications/desktop/notifications-shows-active-snooze-remaining-4.png "Snooze Notifications 🔕 · Desktop")

</Tab>

## What happens while notifications are snoozed?

While a snooze is active, Keet:

* Does **not** show native push notifications for incoming messages.
* Does **not** show in-app notification banners or toasts for incoming messages.
* Does **not** play notification sounds for incoming messages.
* Does **not** play call ringtone notifications.

Snooze never blocks message delivery, message sync, room updates, or app usage - you'll still see everything as normal once you open a chat.

<Callout type="warn">
Snooze pauses alerts only - it doesn't mute individual chats. To keep ignoring a specific group after snooze ends, mute it separately from that group's settings.
</Callout>

## How do I turn snooze off early?

Go back to '**Notifications and sounds**' (desktop) or '**Notifications**' (mobile) and toggle '**Snooze all notifications**' off. Notifications immediately go back to behaving according to your existing notification settings.

Snooze also turns off on its own once the selected duration ends - you don't need to do anything.

## Does snooze sync across my linked devices?

Yes. Since snooze is saved to your profile, it propagates to any [linked device](/installation-and-setup/link-devices) using the same profile, as long as that device supports profile snooze. If a linked device doesn't support it yet, that device simply keeps its previous notification behavior - your other notification settings won't be affected.
