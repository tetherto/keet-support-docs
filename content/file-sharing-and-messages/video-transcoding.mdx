## Why does Keet say a video may not play on all devices?

Some videos are recorded with a codec (commonly **H.265 / HEVC**) that isn't supported on every device or operating system. If a peer's device can't decode that codec, the video simply won't play for them.

Keet can't fix this on the receiving end, so instead it offers to convert the video to a widely compatible format **before you send it** - as long as your own device can play the video.

<Callout type="info">
Conversion happens on your device before sending. Peers who already support the original codec aren't affected either way. 🎞️
</Callout>

## Convert a video before sending

When you attach a video Keet has detected as potentially incompatible, a banner appears above the preview.

### One incompatible video

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

Attach the video - a banner reads '**This video may not play on all devices**'. Select '**Convert video**' to create a compatible version.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

Attach the video - a banner reads '**This video may not play on all devices**'. Tap '**Convert video**' to create a compatible version.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

### Multiple incompatible videos

If you attach several videos at once, affected ones are marked with a warning badge in the thumbnail strip, and the banner covers all of them together.

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

'**Some videos may not play on all devices**' - select '**Convert videos**' to convert all flagged videos at once.

![Some videos may not play on all devices - flagged thumbnails and a 'Convert videos' button.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/video-transcoding/desktop/attach-multiple-videos-shows-may-not-play-banner-2.png "Convert Incompatible Videos 🎞️ · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

'**Some videos may not play on all devices**' - tap '**Convert videos**' to convert all flagged videos at once.

![Some videos may not play on all devices - flagged thumbnails and a 'Convert videos' button.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/video-transcoding/mobile/attach-multiple-videos-shows-may-not-play-banner-2.png "Convert Incompatible Videos 🎞️ · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info">
Don't want to convert? Dismiss the banner [X] and the video sends unaltered - your peers who support the original codec will still be able to play it normally.
</Callout>

If conversion fails for one of several videos, Keet lets you know which ones are ready and gives you the choice to retry the failed one or send it as is.

<Tab value="Desktop">

'**Couldn't convert 1 video. The other video is ready.**' Select '**Retry**', or dismiss to send it unconverted.

## When a video is too large to convert

Very large videos can take a long time to convert, so Keet skips conversion for them and lets you choose how to proceed.

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

'**This video is too large to be converted.**' Send it as is, or compress it outside Keet and try again.

![This video is too large to be converted - send as is, or compress and try again.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/video-transcoding/desktop/attach-video-too-large-to-convert.png "Convert Incompatible Videos 🎞️ · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

'**This video is too large to be converted.**' Send it as is, or compress it outside Keet and try again.

![This video is too large to be converted - send as is, or compress and try again.](/images/file-sharing-and-messages/video-transcoding/mobile/attach-video-too-large-to-convert.png "Convert Incompatible Videos 🎞️ · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="warn">
Sending a video without converting it doesn't break anything - it's simply not guaranteed to play on every peer's device. ⚠️
</Callout>
