## What is Keet?

**Keet** is a peer-to-peer (P2P) communication tool that lets you chat, make audio and video calls, and share media. All 
interactions occur directly between users without intermediaries, so there are no central servers.

## Is Keet encrypted?

Yes, Keet uses **end-to-end encryption**. This means only the people involved in the conversation can see, hear, and access all the shared data.

## How does Keet work without central servers?

Your data is stored and sent P2P: directly between user devices. Because Keet is decentralized and doesn't rely on central servers, it has no single 
point of failure. This makes it more resilient to outages and attacks.

## Is Keet free to use?

Yes, Keet is free across all platforms with no subscription, no hidden fees, and no sponsored posts or ads.

## What is the difference between Keet and traditional messaging apps?

Keet is a messaging app built for digital freedom. It's different on many levels:

| Traditional messengers                                                                           | Keet                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Route traffic through **centralized servers**, creating single points of control and dependency  | 📱📲 Fully P2P / **peer-to-peer**. Messages are sent directly between users without touching the cloud                                       |
| Some encrypt content, but often also **collect metadata** and usage patterns                     | 🔒 End-to-end [encryption](/security-and-privacy) always on —  no hidden metadata, no phone numbers. Your conversations remain yours         |
| Require **phone numbers**, **email addresses**, or other identifiers at signup                   | 🔑 [Create an account](/installation-and-setup/setup) without personal details. Recover with a 24-word key                                   |
| Impose **file size limits**, compress media, and slow down transfers                             | 🔖 [Share files](/file-sharing-and-messages/sharing-files-and-media) of any size directly — no compression                                   |
| Often funded by **ads**, **subscriptions**, or data-driven business models                       | 🆓 **No ads**. **No subscriptions**. No upsell: just secure, private communication for everyone                                              |

## Under the hood

Keet is powered by the innovative open-source[ Pear Runtime ](https://docs.pears.com/)🍐 technology 
stack, which ensures that everything, from operational mechanisms to feature integrations, is peer-to-peer and modifiable.
