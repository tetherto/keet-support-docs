## Why do I need to grant Keet permissions?

Keet may ask for certain permissions depending on your device. These are needed to support core features like messaging, calling, and file sharing. Granting permissions ensures the app works smoothly:

* **Notifications** — so you don’t miss messages, calls, or video chats.
* **Microphone & Camera** — for voice and video calls.
* **Nearby devices** — to connect with Bluetooth headphones, speakers, etc.
* **Media access** — to upload and download files.
* **Screen & System recording** — for sharing your screen when needed.

<Callout type="info">
If you experience issues with certain features, make sure the required permissions are enabled in your device settings. See the [Troubleshooting](/technical-support-and-troubleshooting) section to find out more 🔎
</Callout>
