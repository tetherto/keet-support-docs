## Can I use Keet on multiple devices?

## Current device

Open Keet and go to the '**Profile'** / '**Settings'** / '**My devices'.**

![Open Keet and go to the 'Profile' / 'Settings' / 'My devices'.](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/keet-profile-settings-my-devices.png "Link Devices")

At the top, you’ll see your current device, with any linked devices listed below.

Click ‘**Add a device**’ to generate a pairing link or a QR code.

![Click ‘Add a device’ to generate a pairing link or a QR code.](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/add-device-generate-pairing-link-2.jpg "Link Devices")

On the next screen, you can either:

* Copy the profile link and share it with the other device, or
* Use the QR code to scan later.

![Use the QR code to scan later.](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/qr-code-scan-later-3.png "Link Devices")

Continue the setup on your new device.

## New device

Open **Keet** on the new device and select '**I’m a current user'**.

![Open Keet on the new device and select 'I’m a current user'.](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/keet-new-device-current-user-4.jpg "Link Devices")

On the next screen, choose '**Link device'**.

![On the next screen, choose 'Link device'.](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/next-screen-choose-link-device-5.jpg "Link Devices")

Read the information and, if acceptable, tick the '**I acknowledge**' box and click '**Start device link**'.

![Read the information and, if acceptable, tick the 'I acknowledge' box and click 'Start device link'.](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/read-information-acceptable-tick-acknowledge-6.jpg "Link Devices")

Select either '**Scan QR code'** or '**Use account link'**.

![Select either 'Scan QR code' or 'Use account link'.](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/either-scan-qr-code-account-7.jpg "Link Devices")

* If scanning: scan the QR code generated on your existing device.
* If using the link: paste the link copied from your existing device.

## Confirm Setup

A confirmation message will be sent to the **existing** device. 

Click ‘**Confirm Sync Devices**’ to complete the process.

![If using the link: paste the link copied from your existing device.](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/confirm-setup-using-link-paste-link-copied-8.png "Link Devices · Confirm Setup")

Your chat rooms and settings will now sync across devices.

![If using the link: paste the link copied from your existing device.](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/confirm-setup-using-link-paste-link-copied-9.jpg "Link Devices · Confirm Setup")

## Rename devices

For convenience, you can rename your devices.

To rename your **current** device, go to  '**Profile'** / '**Settings'** / '**My devices'.** Click \[...] / '**Rename Device**' and enter a new name.

![To rename your current device, go to 'Profile' / 'Settings' / 'My devices'. Click ... / 'Rename Device' and….](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/rename-current-device-profile-settings-10.png "Link Devices")

To rename other devices, repeat this process from their own '**My devices'** menu.

![To rename other devices, repeat this process from their own 'My devices' menu.](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/rename-other-devices-repeat-process-11.png "Link Devices")

## Remove linked device

You can unlink the device on which you no longer use Keet by removing it from the Device list.

![You can unlink the device on which you no longer use Keet by removing it from the Device list.](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/unlink-device-which-no-longer-12.png "Link Devices")

Go to  '**Profile'** / '**Settings'** / '**My devices'.** 
Look for the linked device you want to delete, click \[...] / '**Remove device**' and confirm removal:

![Look for the linked device you want to delete, click ... / 'Remove device' and confirm removal:.](/images/installation-and-setup/link-devices/look-linked-device-want-delete-13.png "Link Devices")

<Callout type="info">
When you remove a linked device, you’ll no longer be able to access your profile, shared media, or rooms from it. You can create a new profile on that device or link it again as described in this article.
</Callout>
