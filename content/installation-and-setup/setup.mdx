## How can I create a profile on Keet?

Creating a unique username is optional and will help other users to find you and send DM[^1] requests or [group chat invitations](/keet-groups/joining-groups).

Follow these steps to create your Keet profile:

## Download & Open Keet

Download and install Keet using the [installation guide](/installation-and-setup/installation).

On launch, select “I’m new to Keet. Set up a private profile”. 

![On launch, select “I’m new to Keet. Set up a private profile”.](/images/installation-and-setup/setup/download-keet-launch-new-keet-set-up.png "Setup · Download & Open Keet")

## Set up a recovery phrase

A recovery phrase is a 24-word code that works as a password. If you lose your device or reinstall the app, you can use it to restore your profile and messages.

<Callout type="info">
Setting a pass phrase is a critical step, as it’s the only way to **recover your account** or move it to a new device. 🔏
</Callout>

* **Quick setup**
  Automatically generates a recovery phrase and securely stores it on your device.

![Setup screenshot 2.](/images/installation-and-setup/setup/quick-setup-2.png "Setup")

* **Manual setup**
  Allows you to write down and manage the recovery phrase yourself.

<Callout type="warn">
In case of Manual setup, you should copy or write down the recovery phrase immediately and store it securely. 🔑
</Callout>

![Manual setup.](/images/installation-and-setup/setup/manual-setup-3.png "Setup")

Learn more about recovery phrases and account recovery by the [link](/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/profile-recovery).

## Choose a display name

Your display name is what other users will see in groups and private chats. 

A display name can be changed at any time.

![Manual setup.](/images/installation-and-setup/setup/choose-display-name-manual-setup-4.png "Setup · Choose a display name")

## Choose a username (optional, but recommended)

Your username is how others can find you on Keet. You can choose your username now, or skip and add one later. 

![Manual setup.](/images/installation-and-setup/setup/choose-username-optional-but-manual-setup-5.png "Setup · Choose a username (optional, but recommended)")

<Callout type="info">
If you prefer to remain undiscoverable by a username, **skip this step.**

Just remember: other users won’t be able to find you unless you share your profile QR code with them 🔎
</Callout>

![Manual setup.](/images/installation-and-setup/setup/choose-username-optional-but-manual-setup-6.png "Setup · Choose a username (optional, but recommended)")

You can learn more about usernames in the [Keet Username](/installation-and-setup/keet-username) section.

## Complete setting up

![Manual setup.](/images/installation-and-setup/setup/complete-setting-up-manual-setup-7.png "Setup · Complete setting up")

Once your profile is created, click '**Finish** **setup'** to move forward.

## Feel free to add a profile picture to express your personality 

![Manual setup.](/images/installation-and-setup/setup/feel-free-add-profile-manual-setup-8.jpg "Setup · Feel free to add a profile picture to express your personality")

<Callout type="info">
That’s it! 🖍️ You’re ready to go!
</Callout>

You can now [join groups](/keet-groups/joining-groups), [discover communities](/keet-groups/groups-to-join) or [chat peer-to-peer](/voice-and-video-calls/voice-calls) and explore Keet!
