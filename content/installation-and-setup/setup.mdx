## How can I create a profile on Keet?

Setting up Keet takes one screen: choose a display name, optionally pick a username, and agree to the terms. Keet creates your profile and your [recovery phrase](#your-recovery-phrase) for you.

### Download & Open Keet

Download and install Keet using the [installation guide](/installation-and-setup/installation).

When you open Keet for the first time, select '**Get started**'.

### Set up your profile

On '**Set up your profile**', fill in:

* **Display name** (required). This is the name other people see in groups and private chats. You can change it at any time.
* **Username** (optional, but recommended). Your username is how others can find you on Keet. You can skip it now and [add one later](/installation-and-setup/keet-username).

#### Choosing a username

When you select the username field, Keet suggests a few usernames based on your display name, such as `@alice_227`. Select one to use it, or select '**More**' for new suggestions. You can also type your own.

As you type, Keet shows what a username needs:

* At least 3 characters
* At least one letter
* At least one number
* Letters, numbers & underscores only

Then it shows '**Checking availability...**' and '**Available**' when the username is free. If someone already has it, pick another suggestion or try a different name. You can't continue with a username that's invalid or taken, so clear the field if you'd rather skip it.

### Agree and continue

Read the [Terms of Service and Privacy Statement](#terms-of-service-and-privacy-statement) using the links on the screen, then select '**Agree and continue**'. The button only works once you've entered a display name and any username you typed is valid.

Keet shows '**Sit tight! We're setting up your Keet Profile**' while it creates your profile and secures your recovery phrase on your device. This can take a couple of minutes.

#### Terms of Service and Privacy Statement

The '**Terms of Service**' and '**Privacy Statement**' links on '**Set up your profile**' open the full text. On desktop, select '**Done**' to go back to your profile setup. On mobile, they open in your browser, so switch back to Keet when you've read them. Anything you already entered stays there.

### Welcome to Keet!

Once your profile is ready, you see '**Welcome to Keet!**' with two ways to get going:

* '**Start chat**' opens the [Start chat menu](/keet-groups/create-a-group#how-do-i-start-a-chat-in-keet), where you can find people, create a group, or join a community.
* '**Share profile link**' (desktop) or '**Share my profile QR**' (mobile) shows your profile QR code, so others can scan it and start chatting with you.

#### Share your profile

Your profile QR code shows your name and username. On desktop, select '**Copy QR code**' to copy the QR image or '**Copy username**' to copy your username. Keet confirms with '**QR code copied.**' or '**Username copied.**' On mobile, use the share button to send the QR code, or '**Copy username**'.

## Your recovery phrase

A recovery phrase is a 24-word code that works as a password. If you lose your device or reinstall the app, you can use it to restore your profile and messages.

By default, Keet creates your recovery phrase and stores it securely on your device while it sets up your profile. You can view it later and [back up your profile](/installation-and-setup/back-up-your-profile) to an encrypted file from your profile's recovery phrase settings.

<Callout type="info">
Your recovery phrase is the only way to **recover your account** or move it to a new device. 🔏 Learn more in [Recover a Profile](/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/recover-a-profile).
</Callout>

### Manage my own recovery phrase

If you'd rather write the phrase down yourself, select '**Manage my own recovery phrase**' on '**Set up your profile**', then '**Continue**' on '**Manual setup**'. After you choose your display name and agree to the terms, Keet shows '**Your recovery phrase**'. Write down all 24 words in order, tick the box to confirm you've stored them safely, and select '**Verify phrase**' to check them.

After you verify the phrase, Keet shows '**Backup your phrase**'. Because your phrase isn't saved on the device in manual setup, Keet recommends a backup file: select '**Create backup file**' to save an encrypted copy of your phrase, protected by a password you choose. Select '**Finish setup**' when you're done, or to skip the backup. Learn more in [Back Up Your Profile](/installation-and-setup/back-up-your-profile).

<Callout type="warn">
In manual setup, Keet doesn't keep a copy of your recovery phrase. Copy or write it down immediately and store it somewhere safe. 🔑
</Callout>

## I updated Keet. Do I need to set up my profile again?

No. If you already have a profile, updating Keet doesn't take you through setup again. Your profile, chats, and recovery phrase stay as they are.
