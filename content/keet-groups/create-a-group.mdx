You can create and join as many groups as you want. They can be [completely private](/file-sharing-and-messages/personal-group), for a small company, family or friends, or they can grow into a large and vibrant community with thousands of members. 💫

<Callout type="info">
Keet uses peer-to-peer (P2P) messaging, which means your data travels directly between users. 
No servers, no middlemen.
</Callout>

## How do I start a chat in Keet?

Select '**Start chat**' \[📝] next to the search field at the top of your chat list. You'll also find a '**Start chat**' button on the welcome screen when you have no chats yet.

The '**Start chat**' menu has everything you need to start talking to someone:

* **Search name or username** finds people by their name or [username](/installation-and-setup/keet-username) so you can send them a [DM request](/file-sharing-and-messages/messaging-on-keet#dm-request).
* '**New group chat**' creates a group. See the steps below.
* '**Find people nearby**' finds Keet users close to you over Bluetooth. See [Chat over Bluetooth](/file-sharing-and-messages/chat-over-bluetooth).
* '**Import QR code**' (desktop) reads someone's profile QR code from an image, so you can start a chat with them. '**Scan QR code**' (mobile) reads a profile QR code or a group QR code with your camera, or from an image.
* '**Discover communities**' lists public communities you can join. See [Joining Groups](/keet-groups/joining-groups#where-do-i-search-for-groups-to-join).

### Create a group

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

1. Click '**Start chat**' \[📝] and select '**New group chat**'.
2. Enter a group name. You can also add a description or turn on '**Broadcast Feed (Read-only)**':

   ![Enter a group name and, if you like, a description.](/images/keet-groups/create-a-group/desktop/new-group-chat.png "Create a Group 📲 · Desktop")
3. Click '**Create group chat**'.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

1. Tap '**Start chat**' \[📝] and select '**New group chat**'.
2. Enter a group name. You can also add a description or turn on '**Broadcast Feed (Read-only)**':

   ![Enter a group name and, if you like, a description.](/images/keet-groups/create-a-group/mobile/new-group-chat.png "Create a Group 📲 · Mobile")
3. Tap '**Create**' at the top right.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

The group appears in your chat list, and you're its **Administrator**.

### Invite people to your new group

When the group opens, Keet shows '**You started this group chat**'. Select '**Create & copy invite link**' to create the group's invite link and copy it, so you can paste it to the people you want to invite. Keet confirms with '**Invite link copied to clipboard**', and the button changes to '**Copy invite link**' so you can copy it again later.

## Group Settings

As a group administrator, you control the content and activity within your group. You can manage permissions for calls, messages, links, and file sharing — all settings can be changed at any time.

To access these options, open your group and go to **Group Options / Group Settings**.

Here you can:

* **Enable messages** 
  Toggle the ability for Peers to send text messages and files. Admins and Moderators can still post.
* **Show group activity**
  Toggle system messages in the chat, such as when users join or leave the group, or when the group name changes.
* **Enable link sharing**
  Disable this to prevent members from sending or forwarding messages that contain links for all group members.
* **Enable file sharing**
  Disable this to restrict sending and forwarding of files, media, and voice messages for all group members.
* **Enable calls** 
  Toggle the ability to make calls. Disable this to turn off calling for all group members.

## How big a public group could be?

There’s no limit to the number of peers a group can have.
For example, our public [Keet Welcome](/community-and-support) group has over **20,000 members**, and the [Keet News](/community-and-support) broadcast feed has more than **52,000 peers**.
