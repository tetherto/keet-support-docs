## What are the group member roles in Keet?

In Keet, each group chat is an independent space with **peers** and **admins** and **moderators** who define the rules.

<Callout type="info">
The group creator is automatically assigned the role of Administrator 👑
</Callout>

**Admins** and **Moderators** can restrict a user's access to their group, but this doesn't affect the other users' ability to start their own chats or join others.

![Admins and Moderators can restrict a user's access to their group, but this doesn't affect the other users'….](/images/keet-groups/group-member-roles/admins-moderators-restrict-users-access.png "Group Member Roles 🥷")

### Peer

* Can send messages and media, start group calls (if enabled).
* Can delete their own messages.
* Can invite others to join this group as Peers.
* Can report inappropriate content.

### Moderator

* Can send messages and media, start group calls (if enabled).
* Can change the group name, description and avatar.
* Can pin text and media messages for all members.
* Can delete any messages in the group.
* Can mute/unmute[^1] other peers.
* Can enable/disable calls in the group.
* Can enable/disable room activity events [^2]
* Can promote and invite others to join the group as a Moderator.
* Can report inappropriate content.
* Can see reported content and take appropriate actions.
* Can see reported users and take appropriate actions.
* Can remove users from the group.

### Administrator

* Can send messages and media, start group calls (if enabled).
* Can change the group name, description and avatar.
* Can pin text and media messages for all members.
* Can delete any messages in the group.
* Can mute/unmute[^1] other peers.
* Can enable/disable calls in the group.
* Can enable/disable room activity events [^2]
* Can change the role of any member, including promoting members to Moderator or Admin.
* Can invite others to join as group Administrators.
* Can report inappropriate content.
* Can see reported content and take appropriate actions.
* Can see reported users and take appropriate actions.
* Can remove users from the group.
* Have a responsibility to be frequently online — this helps new members and returning devices catch up on the group quickly.

<Callout type="info">
Learn more about [Administrator responsibilities](/keet-groups/admin-responsibilities).
</Callout>

## How to change the roles of group members?

You get a role when you join a group, based on the invite you used. An Admin or Moderator can change it later from the group's info. You don't need a new invite, and the member doesn't need to leave and rejoin.

Open the group's info, scroll to the list of members, and select a member. Their '**Permissions**' page shows their current role (marked '**Current**') and the roles you can give them under '**Assign role**':

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

Click the group name at the top of the chat to open '**Group info**', then select a member from the members list:

![Select a member from the members list in Group info.](/images/keet-groups/group-member-roles/desktop/members-list.png "Group Member Roles 🥷 · Desktop")

On the '**Permissions**' page, select a role to see what it allows. The button at the bottom of the card ('**Make Peer**', '**Make Moderator**' or '**Make Admin**') applies the change:

![The Permissions page shows the member's current role and the roles you can assign.](/images/keet-groups/group-member-roles/desktop/permissions-peer.png "Group Member Roles 🥷 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

Open the group, tap its name to open '**Group info**', and tap a member in the members list. On the '**Permissions**' screen, tap a role to see what it allows, then tap the button at the bottom of its card ('**Make Peer**', '**Make Moderator**' or '**Make Admin**'):

![On the Permissions screen, expand a role and tap its Make button to change the member's role.](/images/keet-groups/group-member-roles/mobile/permissions-make-admin.png "Group Member Roles 🥷 · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info">

</Callout>

### Upgrade Peer to Moderator

* Open the member's '**Permissions**' page.
* Expand '**Moderator**' and select '**Make Moderator**'. The change applies right away.

### Downgrade Moderator to Peer

* Open the member's '**Permissions**' page.
* Expand '**Peer**', select '**Make Peer**', and confirm with '**Downgrade**'. It removes the moderator's capabilities from the member.
* Moderators can also downgrade themselves in the same way if they decide not to moderate the group anymore.

On mobile, the confirmation looks like this:

![Keet asks you to confirm before it downgrades a member to Peer.](/images/keet-groups/group-member-roles/mobile/downgrade-to-peer-sheet.png "Group Member Roles 🥷 · Mobile")

### Downgrade Admin to Peer or Moderator

* Open the Admin's '**Permissions**' page.
* Expand '**Peer**' or '**Moderator**' and select '**Make Peer**' or '**Make Moderator**'. On desktop, Keet asks you to confirm with '**Downgrade**'. On mobile, it asks only when you downgrade to Peer.
* A group always needs an Admin. If you're the only Admin, Keet shows '**You are the only admin in the group. To change your role, assign another admin first.**' and you can't change your own role until you make someone else an Admin.

  ![If you're the only Admin, Keet asks you to assign another Admin first.](/images/keet-groups/group-member-roles/desktop/only-admin-notice.png "Group Member Roles 🥷 · Desktop")
* Administrators can also downgrade themselves in the same way if they decide not to be an admin and not to moderate the group anymore.

On desktop, the confirmation looks like this:

![Keet asks you to confirm before it downgrades a member.](/images/keet-groups/group-member-roles/desktop/downgrade-to-moderator-modal.png "Group Member Roles 🥷 · Desktop")

### Upgrade a member to an Admin

An Admin can promote any existing member to Admin from their '**Permissions**' page. The member doesn't need a new invite, and they don't need to leave and rejoin the group.

1. Open the member's '**Permissions**' page.
2. Expand '**Admin**'. The card lists what Admins can do. On desktop, it also reminds you that Admins must be frequently online.
3. Select '**Make Admin**'.

The change applies right away, and Keet doesn't ask you to confirm it. Make sure it's the right person before you select the button.

#### Invite someone to a group as an Admin

If the person isn't in the group yet, you can still invite them directly as an Admin:

* As an Administrator, open the group's info and select '**Share invite link**'.
* Set the invite type to '**Admin**', review and accept the Admin rules, then select '**Generate new link**'.
* [Share the invite link with](/keet-groups/joining-groups) the user or let them scan the generated QR code to join as an Administrator.

<Callout type="info">
Keep in mind that an Admin invite link is **single-use** and expires after the set duration
</Callout>

[^1]: the user will not be able to send messages in the chat and will not be notified that they are muted.
    Admins and Mods can't be muted.

[^2]: displaying when peers enter/left the chat room
