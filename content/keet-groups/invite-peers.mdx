Private groups are not searchable or publicly listed in Keet, which helps keep them private and secure. (Public groups and broadcast feeds can be found via in-app search — see [Joining Groups](/keet-groups/joining-groups).)

To bring people in, share the group's **invite link**. Each group has one invite link at a time:

* Anyone can use it, as many times as needed, and it doesn't expire.
* People who join with it become **Peers**. You can [change their role](/keet-groups/group-member-roles) after they join.
* It keeps working until an Admin or Moderator revokes it.

<Callout type="info">
Keet doesn't create a link automatically. An Admin or Moderator creates it the first time it's needed.
</Callout>

## Create the invite link

Admins and Moderators can create the link in two places:

* **Right after you create a group:** select '**Create & copy invite link**' in the chat. See [Create a Group](/keet-groups/create-a-group#how-do-i-start-a-chat-in-keet).
* **From the group's info:** open the group, select the group name at the top to open '**Group info**', and under '**Share link**' select '**Create invite link**'.

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

On desktop, you can also click the three dots '**[...]**' next to '**Share link**' and choose '**Create invite**'.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

![In Group info, tap Create invite link under Share link.](/images/keet-groups/invite-peers/mobile/group-info-create-link.png "Invite Peers 🙋 · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Keet creates the link and shows it under '**Share link**'. When you use '**Create & copy invite link**', Keet also copies it and shows '**Invite link copied to clipboard**'.

If the group already has a link, you see that link instead. Keet never creates a second link while one is active.

## Share the invite link

Open '**Group info**' again whenever you need the link. It shows the group's current link under '**Share link**'.

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

Click the link to copy it, or click the three dots '**[...]**' next to it:

![Click the three dots next to the link to copy it, share it, show its QR code, or revoke it.](/images/keet-groups/invite-peers/desktop/share-link-menu.png "Invite Peers 🙋 · Desktop")

* '**Copy link**' copies the link.
* '**Share link**' opens '**Share group link**', where you choose the kind of link to share, copy it, or share it to Threads or X.
* '**QR code**' shows the group's QR code, which you can copy or share.
* '**Revoke invite**' turns the link off. Only Admins and Moderators see it.

![Share group link lets you pick Keet Link or Web link, copy it, or share it to other apps.](/images/keet-groups/invite-peers/desktop/share-group-link.png "Invite Peers 🙋 · Desktop")

![The group QR code, with Copy QR and share options.](/images/keet-groups/invite-peers/desktop/group-qr-code.png "Invite Peers 🙋 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

Tap the link to open '**Share Group Link**', or tap the QR code icon next to it to show the group's QR code.

![Share Group Link lets you pick Keet link or Web link, share it, copy it, or revoke it.](/images/keet-groups/invite-peers/mobile/share-group-link.png "Invite Peers 🙋 · Mobile")

* '**Share link**' opens your phone's share sheet.
* '**Copy link**' copies the link.
* '**Revoke link**' turns the link off. Only Admins and Moderators see it.

### Keet link or web link?

* A **Keet link** starts with `keet://`. The person you send it to pastes it into the '**Search or join with a link**' field in Keet.
* A **web link** opens in a web browser and takes the person into Keet, or helps them install it first. It's the easiest choice if you're not sure they have Keet. See [Invite Links](/installation-and-setup/invite-links).

Both open the same group, on desktop and mobile. Keet confirms with a message such as '**Group invite link copied**' when you copy one.

### Who can share the link?

Admins and Moderators can always share it. Peers can share it too, as long as the group allows it. If an Admin has turned off group invites in '**Group settings**', Peers see '**Disabled by admin**' instead of the link. If no link has been created yet, Peers see '**No invite available**'.

## Revoke the invite link

Revoke the link if it ended up somewhere you don't want, or when you want to stop new people from joining. Only Admins and Moderators can do this.

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

1. In '**Group info**', click the three dots '**[...]**' next to the link and choose '**Revoke invite**'.
2. Click '**Revoke**' to confirm:

   ![Keet asks you to confirm before it revokes the link.](/images/keet-groups/invite-peers/desktop/revoke-invite.png "Invite Peers 🙋 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

1. In '**Group info**', tap the link, then tap '**Revoke link**'.
2. Tap '**Revoke**' to confirm:

   ![Keet asks you to confirm before it revokes the link.](/images/keet-groups/invite-peers/mobile/revoke-invite.png "Invite Peers 🙋 · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Keet shows '**Group invite link revoked**'. After that:

* Nobody can join with the old link, or with its QR code.
* Everyone already in the group stays in the group.
* The group has no invite link until an Admin or Moderator creates a new one with '**Create invite link**'. The new link replaces the old one, and the old one stays revoked.

## Cannot join the group?

* If the link was revoked, it won't let anyone in. Someone who opens it may stay on '**Waiting for someone to let you in**'. Ask an Admin or Moderator of the group for the current link.
* Someone in the group has to be online to let a new member in. If you're waiting to join, keep Keet open and try again later.
* If the link still doesn't work, try the QR code instead, or ask for a new link.

## What if my peer doesn't have Keet installed?

They don't need to install it first. Send them the **web link** or the QR code. It guides them to download Keet, and once they open the app, they're taken straight into the group — see [Invite Links](/installation-and-setup/invite-links) for the full flow.

## What about groups created with older versions of Keet?

The single invite link needs Keet 4.26 or later. A group that hasn't moved to it yet keeps using the previous invite links, which expire after a set time. On desktop, '**Share link**' in those groups creates a Peer invite link that's valid for 2 days.
