Groups are not searchable or publicly listed in Keet, which helps keep them private and secure.

To generate an invite, open your group and click '**Add Member**':

![To generate an invite, open your group and click 'Add Member':.](/images/keet-groups/invite-peers/generate-invite-group-add-member.png "Invite Peers 🙋")

#### **Choose the expiration period**

Select how long the invite will remain valid. For security reasons, it cannot be used after it expires.

![Select how long the invite will remain valid. For security reasons, it cannot be used after it expires.](/images/keet-groups/invite-peers/choose-expiration-period-how-long-invite-will-remain-2.png "Invite Peers 🙋 · Choose the expiration period")

#### Share group invite link

Send the invite link to your contacts using any communication channel.
They can join by pasting the link into the **'Search or join with a link'** field in Keet.

![They can join by pasting the link into the 'Search or join with a link' field in Keet.](/images/keet-groups/invite-peers/share-group-invite-link-they-join-pasting-link-search-3.png "Invite Peers 🙋 · Share group invite link")

#### Share group QR code

You can also ask your contact to scan the **QR code** using the in-app QR scanner on their mobile device.

![You can also ask your contact to scan the QR code using the in-app QR scanner on their mobile device.](/images/keet-groups/invite-peers/share-group-qr-code-ask-contact-scan-qr-code-4.jpg "Invite Peers 🙋 · Share group QR code")

### Cannot join the group?

If you're facing issues joining the group using the invite link, please ask your peer to generate a new invitation. If possible, try joining the QR code instead.
