### How do I join a group?

### Joining with an Invite link

When you receive an invite link from a peer (keet://...) , just click it or copy & paste it into the search bar:

![When you receive an invite link from a peer (keet://...) , just click it or copy & paste it into the search….](/images/keet-groups/joining-groups/receive-invite-link-peer-keet.png "Joining Groups 🛋️")

### Join with a QR Code

You can use your camera to scan a QR code or upload it as a file. Here's how:

On your mobile device, tap the ‘\[🏁]’ button on the top right of the screen, allow access to your camera and scan a QR code. 

![On your mobile device, tap the ‘🏁’ button on the top right of the screen, allow access to your camera and ….](/images/keet-groups/joining-groups/mobile-device-button-top-right-2.jpg "Joining Groups 🛋️")

You can also upload the QR code if you cannot access or don't have a camera on your mobile device.

In Keet, tap the ‘\[🏁]’ button on top of the screen, and tap the '\[📸]' button in the bottom left. Allow access to media files and upload a QR code. 

![In Keet, tap the ‘🏁’ button on top of the screen, and tap the '📸' button in the bottom left. Allow access….](/images/keet-groups/joining-groups/keet-button-top-screen-button-3.jpg "Joining Groups 🛋️")

### Where do I search for groups to join?

At the moment, groups in Keet can only be joined via an invite link or QR code — they aren’t searchable or publicly listed.
You can access groups through invitations from other peers or by following shared links to [public communities](/keet-groups/groups-to-join).

### How to join public groups?

If you just created the profile, you can click '**Discover Communities**' to enter the public group: 

![If you just created the profile, you can click 'Discover Communities' to enter the public group:.](/images/keet-groups/joining-groups/created-profile-discover-communities-enter-4.png "Joining Groups 🛋️")

Select the groups that may be of interest to you and click '**Join'**:

![Select the groups that may be of interest to you and click 'Join':.](/images/keet-groups/joining-groups/groups-may-interest-join-5.png "Joining Groups 🛋️")

You can also find these and other groups by the link [Groups to Join 🧩](/keet-groups/groups-to-join).
