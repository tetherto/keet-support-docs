## How do I join a group?

### Joining with an Invite link

When you receive an invite link from a peer (keet://...) , just click it or copy & paste it into the search bar:

![When you receive an invite link from a peer (keet://...) , just click it or copy & paste it into the search….](/images/keet-groups/joining-groups/receive-invite-link-peer-keet.png "Joining Groups 🛋️")

<Callout type="info">
Don't have Keet yet? See [Invite Links](/installation-and-setup/invite-links) to learn what happens when you click a room link, scan a room QR code, or scan a profile QR code before installing the app.
</Callout>

### Join with a QR Code

On mobile, you can use your camera to scan a group QR code or upload it as an image. You'll also find '**Scan QR code**' in the '**Start chat**' menu.

<Callout type="info">
On desktop, '**Import QR code**' in the '**Start chat**' menu only reads profile QR codes. To join a group on desktop, use the group's invite link instead.
</Callout>

Here's how on mobile:

On your mobile device, tap the ‘\[🏁]’ button on the top right of the screen, allow access to your camera and scan a QR code. 

![On your mobile device, tap the ‘🏁’ button on the top right of the screen, allow access to your camera and ….](/images/keet-groups/joining-groups/mobile-device-button-top-right-2.jpg "Joining Groups 🛋️")

You can also upload the QR code if you cannot access or don't have a camera on your mobile device.

In Keet, tap the ‘\[🏁]’ button on top of the screen, and tap the '\[📸]' button in the bottom left. Allow access to media files and upload a QR code. 

![In Keet, tap the ‘🏁’ button on top of the screen, and tap the '📸' button in the bottom left. Allow access….](/images/keet-groups/joining-groups/keet-button-top-screen-button-3.jpg "Joining Groups 🛋️")

## Where do I search for groups to join?

Public groups and broadcast feeds are searchable and discoverable directly within Keet — use the search bar to find one by name and join it. You can also still join a group through an invite link, QR code, or an invitation from another peer.

## How to join public groups?

Use '**Discover communities**' to browse recommended public groups and broadcast feeds:

1. Select '**Start chat**' \[📝], then '**Discover communities**'. The list opens on '**Recommended for you**'.
2. To join one group, select the join button next to it. To join several at once, select '**Select**', tick the groups you want, and select '**Join**'. '**Join**' stays greyed out until you tick at least one group.
