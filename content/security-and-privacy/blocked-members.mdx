## How do I see the members I've blocked?

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

Click your avatar to open '**Profile**', then go to '**Settings**' / '**Privacy and Security**' and select '**Blocked members**':

![Blocked members lists every member you've blocked, with their avatar and name.](/images/security-and-privacy/blocked-members/desktop/blocked-members-list.png "Blocked Members 🚫 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

Go to '**Profile**' / '**Settings**' / '**Privacy and Security**' and tap '**Blocked members**':

![Blocked members lists every member you've blocked, with their avatar and name.](/images/security-and-privacy/blocked-members/mobile/blocked-members-list.png "Blocked Members 🚫 · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

The list shows each blocked member with their avatar and name, and the number of blocked members above it. If a member has no avatar, Keet shows a letter placeholder instead. If you've given a blocked contact an [alias](/file-sharing-and-messages/contact-book#giving-a-contact-an-alias), mobile shows the alias and desktop shows their public display name.

The list includes everyone you've blocked, whether or not they're in your [Contact Book](/file-sharing-and-messages/contact-book). If you haven't blocked anyone, the screen shows '**No blocked members.**'

<Callout type="info">
🚫 Blocked members can no longer call you or send you messages. They aren't notified when you block them, when you view this list, or when you unblock them.
</Callout>

## How do I unblock a member?

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

1. Open '**Blocked members**' and select the member you want to unblock.
2. On their '**User profile**', select '**Unblock Member**':

   ![On the blocked member's User profile, select Unblock Member.](/images/security-and-privacy/blocked-members/desktop/blocked-member-profile.png "Blocked Members 🚫 · Desktop")
3. Keet asks you to confirm. Select '**Unblock**':

   ![Confirm in the Unblock dialog, or select Cancel to keep the member blocked.](/images/security-and-privacy/blocked-members/desktop/unblock-confirmation.png "Blocked Members 🚫 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

1. Open '**Blocked members**' and tap the member you want to unblock.
2. On their '**User profile**', tap '**Unblock member**':

   ![On the blocked member's User profile, tap Unblock member.](/images/security-and-privacy/blocked-members/mobile/blocked-member-profile.png "Blocked Members 🚫 · Mobile")
3. Keet asks you to confirm. Tap '**Unblock**':

   ![Confirm in the Unblock sheet, or tap Cancel to keep the member blocked.](/images/security-and-privacy/blocked-members/mobile/unblock-confirmation.png "Blocked Members 🚫 · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Keet shows '**You can now chat with**' followed by their name and takes you back to the list, where the member no longer appears. The list updates every time you block or unblock someone.

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

![After you unblock, Keet confirms and the member leaves the list.](/images/security-and-privacy/blocked-members/desktop/unblocked-confirmation.png "Blocked Members 🚫 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

![After you unblock, Keet confirms and the member leaves the list.](/images/security-and-privacy/blocked-members/mobile/unblocked-confirmation.png "Blocked Members 🚫 · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

If you select '**Cancel**' in the confirmation, nothing changes - the member stays blocked and stays in the list.

You can also unblock someone from a group's member list: open their profile and select '**Unblock Member**' (desktop) or '**Unblock member**' (mobile).

## What happens when I unblock someone?

* They can send you a chat request again, and you'll see their messages in the chat again.
* They aren't notified that you unblocked them.
* Unblocking doesn't start a chat, send a request, or add them to your [Contact Book](/file-sharing-and-messages/contact-book). If they were already one of your contacts, they stay saved, with their [alias](/file-sharing-and-messages/contact-book#giving-a-contact-an-alias) if you set one.
