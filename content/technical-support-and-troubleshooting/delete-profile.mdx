## Can I log out or delete my profile from a device?

![Delete Profile screenshot 1.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/delete-profile/screenshot.png "Delete Profile")

Make sure you've saved all necessary data and copied the recovery phrase in case you need to regain access to your profile.

Once ready, tick the 'I understand the data will be erased' checkbox if agreeable.

![Once ready, tick the 'I understand the data will be erased' checkbox if agreeable.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/delete-profile/once-ready-tick-understand-data-2.png "Delete Profile")

<Callout type="warn">
You won't be able to restore the profile unless you have set up and saved your 
[recovery phrase](/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/recover-a-profile).
</Callout>

After confirmation, the data will be wiped out from your **current device** and Keet will restart. 
You can now close the app or [create a new profile](/installation-and-setup/setup).
