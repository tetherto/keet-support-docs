## Does Keet support in-app text translation?

Yes. You can translate received text messages directly inside the chat - see [Translate Messages](/file-sharing-and-messages/translate-messages).

Translation happens locally on your device using downloadable language packs. To see which packs are stored on your device and how much space they use, see [Language Packs](/file-sharing-and-messages/language-packs).
