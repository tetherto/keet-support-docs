### What is a recovery phrase?

You should **securely store** your recovery phrase after the profile setup is complete and not share it with anyone else. 

### How to view the recovery phrase in Keet?

<Callout type="warn">
The recovery phrase can only be viewed on your original device if you selected the '**Quick setup**' option during [profile creation](/installation-and-setup/setup).
</Callout>

Open Keet on the device you created the profile (mobile or desktop). 
Go to '**Profile**' /  '**Settings**' / '**Privacy and Security**' / '**Recovery phrase**'.

![Go to 'Profile' / 'Settings' / 'Privacy and Security' / 'Recovery phrase'.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/profile-recovery/profile-settings-privacy-security-recovery.png "Profile Recovery")

Read the security information and, if applicable, check the box to confirm. 
After that, click '**View phrase**'.

![After that, click 'View phrase'.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/profile-recovery/after-view-phrase-2.png "Profile Recovery")

Write down or copy directly from the app and store it in a safe place.

After viewing and copying the seed phrase, click '**Done**'.

### How can I regain access to my profile if I lose my device?

If you lose your device, you can use another linked device (if any) or your recovery phrase to regain access to your personal profile.

![If you lose your device, you can use another linked device (if any) or your recovery phrase to regain acces….](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/profile-recovery/lose-device-another-linked-device-3.png "Profile Recovery")

**Link to existing identity**
If you have other devices linked with the same Keet profile, you can set up a new device by following the [linking device](/installation-and-setup/link-devices) guide.

**Recover profile**
If no other devices are linked to your Keet profile, you can use recovery phrase.

Install Keet on a new device and select '**I already use Keet**' / '**Recover profile**'

![Install Keet on a new device and select 'I already use Keet' / 'Recover profile'.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/profile-recovery/install-keet-new-device-already-4.png "Profile Recovery")

Use your backup recovery phrase to recover your profile, paste the 24 words or enter them manually.

![Use your backup recovery phrase to recover your profile, paste the 24 words or enter them manually.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/profile-recovery/backup-recovery-phrase-recover-profile-5.png "Profile Recovery")

### Can I have multiple recovery phrases for my profile?

No, each profile is associated with a unique recovery phrase. You cannot have multiple recovery phrases for one account.

### Is it possible to change my seed phrase or get a new one?

No, you cannot change the recovery phrase for your account. Each profile is associated with a unique seed phrase, you cannot generate a new 24-word phrase for the same Keet account.

### Can I generate a recovery phrase offline?

Yes, you can generate the recovery phrase and set up your profile offline.
