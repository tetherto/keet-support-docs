## What is a recovery phrase?

In Keet, the recovery phrase is a human-readable backup of 24 random words that lets you restore your profile if you lose your device or 
reinstall the app. It is a 24-word code that works as a password.
You should **securely store** your recovery phrase after the profile setup is complete and not share it with anyone else.

For an extra layer of protection, you can also [back up your profile](/installation-and-setup/back-up-your-profile) to an 
encrypted file that is unlocked with a password you choose.

## How to view the recovery phrase in Keet?

<Callout type="warn">
The recovery phrase can only be viewed on your original device if you selected the '**Quick setup**' option during 
[profile creation](/installation-and-setup/setup).
</Callout>

Open Keet on the device you created the profile (mobile or desktop). 
Go to '**Profile and Settings**' / '**Settings**' / '**Privacy and Security**' / '**Recovery Phrase**'.

![Go to 'Profile and Settings' / 'Settings' / 'Privacy and Security' / 'Recovery Phrase'.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/recover-a-profile/profile-settings-privacy-security-recovery.png "Recover a Profile")

Read the security information and, if applicable, check the box to confirm. 
After that, click '**View phrase**'.

![After that, click 'View phrase'.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/recover-a-profile/after-view-phrase-2.png "Recover a Profile")

Write down or copy directly from the app and store it in a safe place.

After viewing and copying the seed phrase, click '**Done**'.

## How can I regain access to my profile if I lose my device?

If you lose your device, you can use another linked device (if any) or recover your profile from scratch.

### Link to existing identity

If you have other devices linked with the same Keet profile, set up a new device by following the 
[linking device](/installation-and-setup/link-devices) guide.

### Recover profile

If no other devices are linked to your Keet profile, recover it using your 24-word recovery phrase or an 
[encrypted backup file](/installation-and-setup/back-up-your-profile). Follow the steps below.


On the '**I'm a current user**' screen, choose '**Recover profile**', [instead of '**Link device**' 
(which is for adding a device to a profile you still have access to)](#link-to-existing-identity).

#### Recover with your backup file

1. Choose '**Recover with backup file**'.
2. Attach your encrypted backup file from your device or cloud storage.
3. Enter the **decryption password** you set when creating it.
4. Select '**Recover profile**'.

## Can I have multiple recovery phrases for my profile?

No, each profile is associated with a unique recovery phrase. You cannot have multiple recovery phrases for one account.

## Is it possible to change my seed phrase or get a new one?

No, you cannot change the recovery phrase for your account. Each profile is associated with a unique seed phrase, you cannot 
generate a new 24-word phrase for the same Keet account.

## Can I generate a recovery phrase offline?

Yes, you can generate the recovery phrase and set up your profile offline.
