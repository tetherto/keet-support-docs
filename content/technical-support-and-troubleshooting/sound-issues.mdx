## My contact can't hear me

  ![Unmute the microphone in the call options panel:.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/sound-issues/unmute-microphone-call-options-panel.png "Sound issues")
* Unmute the microphone in the call options panel:<br />

  ![Unmute the microphone in the call options panel:.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/sound-issues/unmute-microphone-call-options-panel-2.png "Sound issues")
* Verify that Keet has the necessary microphone permissions on your device.

- [x] Desktop: '**Settings**' / '**Privacy & Security**' / '**Microphone**' / '**Keet**'
- [x] Mobile: '**Settings**' / '**Apps**' / '**Keet**' / '**Permissions**'

![X Mobile: 'Settings' / 'Apps' / 'Keet' / 'Permissions'.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/sound-issues/mobile-settings-apps-keet-permissions-3.png "Sound issues")

* Please note that after the update or reinstall the OS may request microphone access once again.

![Please note that after the update or reinstall the OS may request microphone access once again.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/sound-issues/please-note-after-update-reinstall-4.png "Sound issues")

* Test the microphone in other apps to rule out hardware issues.

## I can't hear the other person on a call

* Check your device’s volume settings. 
* Verify that your contact has the Microphone enabled and unmuted:

![Verify that your contact has the Microphone enabled and unmuted:.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/sound-issues/verify-contact-has-microphone-enabled-5.png "Sound issues")

* Ensure speakers or headphones are properly connected.
* Restart or re-enter the call if the issue persists.

## Sound quality is low

* Make sure both you and your contact have a stable internet connection.
* Check that the correct audio device is selected:

![Check that the correct audio device is selected:.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/sound-issues/check-correct-audio-device-selected-6.jpg "Sound issues")

* If using Bluetooth headphones, confirm they are connected and [Keet has permission](/installation-and-setup/app-permissions) to access  nearby devices.
* If the issue continues, try restarting or re-entering the call.
