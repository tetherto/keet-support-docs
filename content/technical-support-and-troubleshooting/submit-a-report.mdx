# Submit a Report

### What should I do if I encounter a bug or want to provide a suggestion? 

<Callout type="info">
Keet runs without servers and does not store any information we could use for troubleshooting. 🔐

That’s why your detailed report is essential in helping us understand and resolve the problem.
</Callout>

When reporting a bug in Keet, please include:

* **Device(s)** where the issue occurs (PC / mobile).
* **OS** and **Keet version.**
* **Screenshot or video** of the issue (optional but very helpful).
* **Steps to reproduce** the issue.
* **Frequency** (one-time, occasional, observed under specific conditions/networks/groups, or permanent, etc).

### Send feedback or a feature request

Go to '**Profile' /** '**Feedback'** and select '**Feature**' or '**Suggestions'**.

Tell us your ideas for improving the app.

![](/files/XJLMvxFIYvjI47fhz6F4.png)

<Callout type="info">
We appreciate your help in improving Keet! 💫
</Callout>

### Error log

If you encounter an error, you can send a report immediately or access past errors in the log.

![](/files/kvasvssfc4AxkaPS5c7t.png)


Click '**View details**' to see the error or go to **Profile > Error log** and select the error.

You can copy the details or send a report with any additional information.
