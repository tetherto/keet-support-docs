### I can't use the camera on Keet

![Verify that you have granted Keet the necessary camera permissions.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/video-issues/verify-have-granted-keet-necessary.png "Video issues")

* Verify that you have granted Keet the necessary camera permissions.<br />

- [x] Desktop: '**Settings**' / '**Privacy & Security**' / '**Camera**' / '**Keet**'
- [x] Mobile: '**Settings**' / '**Apps**' / '**Keet**' / '**Permissions**'

![X Mobile: 'Settings' / 'Apps' / 'Keet' / 'Permissions'.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/video-issues/mobile-settings-apps-keet-permissions-2.png "Video issues")

* Test the camera in other apps to ensure it's functioning properly.

### Video quality is low

* Adjust video quality settings during the call on Desktop. 
  Click on the dropdown button next to the camera icon to change video quality.

![Adjust video quality settings during the call on Desktop.](/images/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/video-issues/adjust-video-quality-settings-during-3.png "Video issues")

* Ensure that you and your contact have a stable internet connection.
