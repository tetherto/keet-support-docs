## How do I send a reaction during a call?

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

While in a call, select the reaction icon in the call controls to open the reaction picker, then choose an emoji:

![While in a call, select the reaction icon in the call controls to open the reaction picker, then choose an….](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-reactions/desktop/reaction-picker-bar.png "Call Reactions 🎉 · Desktop")

Select the **+** button for the full emoji picker if you want a reaction outside the quick list:

![Select the + button for the full emoji picker if you want a reaction outside the quick list.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-reactions/desktop/full-emoji-picker.png "Call Reactions 🎉 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

While in a call, tap '**React**' to open the reaction picker, then choose an emoji:

![While in a call, tap 'React' to open the reaction picker, then choose an emoji.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-reactions/mobile/reaction-in-dm-call-mobile.png "Call Reactions 🎉 · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Your reaction appears floating over your video for everyone on the call to see, labeled with your name, then disappears on its own after a moment:

<Tabs items={["Desktop", "Mobile"]}>
<Tab value="Desktop">

![Your reaction appears floating over your video for everyone on the call to see.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-reactions/desktop/reaction-floating-sent.png "Call Reactions 🎉 · Desktop")

</Tab>
<Tab value="Mobile">

![Your reaction appears floating over your video for everyone on the call to see.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-reactions/mobile/reaction-floating-sent-mobile.png "Call Reactions 🎉 · Mobile")

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info">
😄 Reactions don't post a message in the room's chat, and they don't remain on screen permanently - they clear automatically. Leaving and rejoining a call also clears any previous reactions.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
Sending or receiving a reaction doesn't mute or unmute anyone, and doesn't change anyone's camera state. Multiple people can react at the same time without disrupting the call, and every other call control stays usable while reactions are on screen.
</Callout>

Desktop and Mobile share the same reaction set and behavior, including in one-to-one calls:
