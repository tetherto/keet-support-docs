## How do I record and transcribe a call?

This feature is available on **Desktop** only.

Select the record control in the call toolbar. In the dialog that opens, tick '**Start transcription as well**' if you also want a transcript and summary, then select '**Start recording**':

![Select the record control in the call toolbar. In the dialog that opens, tick 'Start transcription as well'….](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/start-recording-confirmation.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

<Callout type="info">
🔒 The recording is processed locally on your device to generate the transcript and summary - it isn't uploaded to a cloud transcription service. The transcription option only appears on devices that support local transcription.
</Callout>

While it's running, a small recording indicator shows in the call header, with a brief '**Transcribing…**' status if transcription is on. The call stays fully usable the whole time.

## How do I stop, and where does the recording go?

Select the record control again. Choose whether to send the result to the room's chat or download it - if you started transcription too, it stops and saves alongside the recording automatically:

![Choose whether to send the result to the room's chat or download it.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/stop-recording-confirmation.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

If you sent it to chat, Keet shows a processing state while the transcript and summary are generated:

![Keet shows a processing state while the transcript and summary are generated.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/processing-toast.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

<Callout type="warn">
If transcript or summary generation fails, your audio recording is unaffected - it stays available to play or download, and Keet offers to recover it if processing was interrupted.
</Callout>

## Where do I find the transcript and summary?

Open a group's '**Group info**' panel and select '**Call records**' to see every recording and transcript for that room:

![Open a group's 'Group info' panel and select 'Call records' to see every recording and transcript for that….](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/group-info-call-records-entry.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

![See every recording and transcript for that room.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/call-records-list.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

Selecting a transcript entry opens the full record, with a summary, action items, and the full transcript. You can select any of the transcript text, and open or download the full transcript from here:

![Selecting a transcript entry opens the full record, with a summary, action items, and the full transcript.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/transcript-detail-view.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

<Callout type="info">
If a speaker can't be identified in the recording, Keet won't guess a name or attribute an action item to the wrong person. If a call has no usable speech, you'll see a clear message that there wasn't enough audio to transcribe or summarize instead of an empty result.
</Callout>

## Can I view a transcript on Mobile?

Not yet as a dedicated transcript view. If a transcript and summary are shared to a room's chat, Mobile currently shows them using its standard file/message display rather than the rich summary-and-transcript view described above - that dedicated Mobile viewer isn't available in this release. Starting or processing a recording/transcription is Desktop-only either way.
