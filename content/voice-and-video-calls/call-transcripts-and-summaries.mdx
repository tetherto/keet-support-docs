## How do I record and transcribe a call?

This feature is available on **Desktop** only.

Select the record control in the call toolbar. In the dialog that opens, tick '**Start transcription as well**' if you also want a transcript and summary, then select '**Start recording**':

![Select the record control in the call toolbar. In the dialog that opens, tick 'Start transcription as well'….](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/start-recording-confirmation.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

<Callout type="info">
🔒 The recording is processed locally on your device to generate the transcript and summary - it isn't uploaded to a cloud transcription service. The transcription option only appears on devices that support local transcription.
</Callout>

While it's running, the call header shows '**Recording…**' and '**Transcribing…**' - the call stays fully usable the whole time:

![While it's running, the call header shows 'Recording…' and 'Transcribing…' - the call stays fully usable the….](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/recording-transcribing-active.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

## How do I stop, and where does it go?

Select the record control again. Choose whether to send the result to the room's chat or download it - if you started transcription too, it stops and saves alongside the recording automatically:

![Choose whether to send the result to the room's chat or download it.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/stop-recording-confirmation.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

<Callout type="warn">
⏱️ Under 5 minutes, Keet warns '**Your audio is very short for a meeting summary, it may not work well for transcription**'. The summary can describe a call that never happened - check it against the recording.
</Callout>

If you send it to chat, the recording itself lands right away as a normal audio message. The transcript and summary take a little longer, so Keet shows a brief preparing notice first, then lets you know once only the transcript is left to finish:

![Keet shows a brief preparing notice, then lets you know once only the transcript is left to finish.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/audio-delivered-transcript-preparing.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

![Keet shows a brief preparing notice, then lets you know once only the transcript is left to finish.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/transcript-preparing-toast.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

<Callout type="warn">
If transcript or summary generation fails, your audio recording is unaffected - it stays available to play or download, and Keet offers to recover it if processing was interrupted.
</Callout>

Once ready, the transcript arrives as its own message with an AI-generated summary shown inline, and a '**Show all**' link to expand it:

![The transcript arrives as its own message with an AI-generated summary shown inline, and a 'Show all' link….](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/transcript-summary-message.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

<Callout type="info">
If a speaker can't be identified in the recording, Keet won't guess a name or attribute an action item to the wrong person. If a call has no usable speech, you'll see a clear message that there wasn't enough audio to transcribe or summarize instead of an empty result.
</Callout>

Opening the attached file shows the full record - a summary, key decisions, action items, and the complete timestamped, speaker-labeled transcript - and its text is selectable:

![Opening the attached file shows the full record - a summary, key decisions, action items, and the complete….](/images/voice-and-video-calls/call-transcripts-and-summaries/desktop/transcript-full-file.png "Call Transcripts & Summaries 📝")

## Can I view a transcript on Mobile?

Starting or processing a recording/transcription is Desktop-only. But since the result is delivered as ordinary chat messages - an audio file, then a transcript file with its summary - Mobile can already open, play, and download them like any other shared file. Mobile doesn't yet render the AI summary card the same way Desktop does.
