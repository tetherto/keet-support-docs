## What is the "You're muted" reminder?

If you start talking during a call while your microphone is muted, Keet shows a small reminder above the microphone button:

> **You're muted.** Click to unmute yourself so that they can hear you.

![A "You're muted" reminder appears above the microphone button when you speak while muted.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/speaking-while-muted/desktop/muted-popup.png "Speaking While Muted 🎙️ · Desktop")

The reminder works in direct calls and in group calls.

<Callout type="info">
The reminder is available on **Keet desktop**. It isn't part of the mobile app in this release.
</Callout>

## When does it appear?

Keet shows the reminder when it hears you talking for about **two seconds** in a row while you're muted. Short pauses between words don't reset the count, but a brief sound, like a click, isn't long enough to trigger it.

You never see the reminder while your microphone is on.

## How do I dismiss it?

The reminder goes away when you:

* Unmute your microphone. Select the microphone button, or use the shortcut shown when you hover over it.
* Select the close button ('**Hide this message**'), select anywhere outside the reminder, or press **Esc**.
* Stop talking. It closes on its own about a minute after you go quiet.

After you dismiss it, Keet doesn't show it again until you unmute and mute yourself again.

## Who can see it, and is my audio recorded?

* **Only you see the reminder.** Other people in the call can't see it.
* **Keet checks on your device.** It listens to your microphone locally to tell when you're talking. While you're muted, nothing is sent to the call, recorded, or stored.

## I don't see the reminder

* Check that Keet has [microphone access](/installation-and-setup/app-permissions) and that the right microphone is selected in the call. If Keet can't use your microphone, it can't tell when you're talking, and the call continues normally.
* The reminder doesn't appear while another Keet message about microphone access is open. Close that message first.
* If you're having trouble with sound during calls, see [Sound issues](/technical-support-and-troubleshooting/sound-issues).
