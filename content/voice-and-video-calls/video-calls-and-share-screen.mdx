## How do I share my screen on Keet?

![Video Calls & Share Screen 🎬 screenshot 1.](/images/voice-and-video-calls/video-calls-and-share-screen/screenshot.png "Video Calls & Share Screen 🎬")

Keet supports multiple people sharing their screens at the same time, making it great for collaborative sessions like team meetings or workshops.

You can enable **Private Mode** before or during a call to hide the chat list and its contents for added privacy.

Before sharing your screen, make sure Keet has the necessary **Screen & System Recording** permissions granted in your device settings. 
Without these permissions, screen sharing may not work properly.

For the best performance, we recommend sharing your screen or using your camera, but not both at once (especially for the presenter). 
Running both can be resource-demanding for your device and may impact performance.
